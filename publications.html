Research and Publications

Peer-reviewed articles, reviews and editorial commentary by Dr. Alexandra Tabakin, as indexed in PubMed.

Dr. Tabakin's published work spans urogynecology, urology residency education, and urologic oncology, with recent papers on overactive bladder therapies, vaginal estrogen prescribing, and urogynecology training. The list below is drawn from PubMed and cross-checked against her ORCID record; conference abstracts and book chapters are not included. 47 articles and reviews (15 as first author) and 10 invited editorial comments and replies, 57 items in all.
